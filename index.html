Hi!

I’m Mihir, an ICT undergraduate at Pandit Deendayal Energy University who loves turning ideas into working hardware and firmware.


I build robots, FPGA designs and IoT systems — from custom ESP32 boards and Verilog state machines to wirelessly controlled walking robots.


Based in Gandhinagar, I like projects where RTL, PCBs, microcontrollers and code all have to work together on a real bench.
