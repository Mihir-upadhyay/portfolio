02   Robotics & firmware

8-DOF Quadruped Robot

A battery-powered four-legged robot with a crawl-gait engine on an ESP32, a PCA9685 servo driver and a handheld joystick over ESP-NOW. About 3.2 ms latency and 42 minutes of runtime.
